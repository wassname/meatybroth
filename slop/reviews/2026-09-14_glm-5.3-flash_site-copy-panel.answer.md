## What the site is

Meatybroth.com is a public reader over a recent snapshot of posts (last 30 days) collected from relays — likely ActivityPub/ATP-style federated posts, given the follow-graph framing. It collects, indexes, and re-presents the corpus for browsing and semantic search, with transparency panels exposing collection coverage and indexing status.

## How the controls work

- **View modes.** *Latest* is a reverse-chronological feed. *Words* is keyword search (supports `"exact phrase"`). *Meaning* is semantic search via a chosen embedding backend (*MiniLM* or *Titan*), returning related posts even without shared words. *With replies* shows threads with recent replies, and a search narrows it to matching threads. *Network* expands your local follow graph (1–3 hops) and surfaces posts from nearby accounts, orderable by recency or connectivity. *Topics* clusters related posts, either into a fixed number of groups or via DBSCAN, then lets you browse per-topic cards.
- **Sort** only makes sense for text/semantic search (Relevance vs Newest); presumably inert in Latest/Topics.
- **Per-post card.** Author, age, and score are display fields; *Replies*, *Event*, *Post details*, and the author link are the actionable elements. *Similar* requests related posts for that post, but only works once indexed — hence the pending notice.

## Transparency sections

*Status* reports coverage ("[count] posts available"), *Collection* shows relay checks with gaps and missing profiles plus raw relay reports, and *Related-post index* shows per-backend readiness/pending counts, model IDs, hashes, and request/cost counts. This is unusually honest about incompleteness — good.

## Misleading or unclear labels

1. **"Find:" vs. the *Words/Meaning* buttons vs. "Search in:"** — three coupled controls (query, mode, backend), but nothing explains that *Meaning* depends on the *Search in* choice while *Words* presumably ignores it. A reader may set a backend and expect it to affect keyword search.
2. **"Sort: Relevance"** — meaningless for Latest or Topics; the reader isn't told when it applies.
3. **Non-linked age/score** looks clickable next to linked author — ambiguous affordance.
4. **"Event"** is unexplained; no help text says what it shows or opens.
5. **"Network: accounts near the people you follow"** — "near" is undefined (hops? count?); and "The follow graph here is incomplete" undercuts the *Order: Most connected* metric, since connectivity is computed over partial data.
6. **"Status" as a link/section name** is vague; the page notice and status both describe the 30-day window redundantly.
7. **Topic label clickability**: "Choose a topic" suggests a selector, but it's unclear whether topic labels/counts in the list are links or buttons.

Missing facts: what *Event* and *Post details* reveal, how score is computed, and whether backend choice persists across searches.
